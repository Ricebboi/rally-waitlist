Community privacy

This page describes Rally Community. See the Rally privacy policy for the rest of the app.

Your account and content

Community supports Apple, Google, an emailed sign-in code, or an existing email/password account. For password sign-in, the app sends the password to Supabase Auth over HTTPS and does not save it; Supabase stores a salted password hash. Supabase handles authentication and stores your account, profile, posts, comments, likes, follow relationships, blocks, reports, and uploaded photos. Joining also saves your four tennis setup answers, beginner preference, adult-confirmation status and agreement version privately to your account for restoration. Your birthdate is not included. Apple and Google passwords stay with those providers. Resend delivers account emails. Rally's Community service and deletion worker run on Modal.

What other people can see

Your username, display name, biography and profile photo can be visible to other unblocked Community members, including when your profile is private. Everyone posts from public profiles can appear in the global feed. Private profiles require accepted followers to view their posts. Only me posts stay visible only to their owner, except for the limited reported-content moderation described below. Blocks restrict discovery and content access. Shared web links do not expose profile or post details.

Photos and safety

Rally processes uploaded photos on its server to check supported formats, limit size, remove metadata, and screen content. Screening can be imperfect. Reporting lets Rally inspect the specific reported content and take action; Founder access does not grant general browsing of unreported private posts.

Your tennis data and subscription

Joining Community does not automatically upload your saved tennis history or photos. You choose what to post. Apple subscriptions and Rally Pro access are separate from Community accounts. Deleting a Community account does not cancel an Apple subscription or delete local tennis history.

Deletion and support

Use Delete community account in Community settings to request deletion of your Community account and associated content. The server processes deletion and provider-revocation work; failed steps may retry. Reports and administrative audit records are limited to their operational purpose and the applicable deletion process. Database backups may retain earlier records for the backup retention period.

Manage or cancel Rally Pro through Apple subscription settings. For privacy requests, reports, or help, contact rallyai.app@gmail.com.
