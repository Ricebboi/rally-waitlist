Community rules

Rally Community is for adults 18 and older. Share tennis, everyday moments, and encouragement respectfully.

  • Only share content you have the right and permission to share.
  • No harassment, hateful abuse, threats, sexual exploitation, explicit sexual content, graphic violence, scams, or spam.
  • Do not publish someone else's private information or photos without permission.
  • Choose your audience carefully. Everyone posts from public profiles can appear in the public feed. Followers and Only me content retain their selected audiences.

Reporting and enforcement

Use Report from a profile, post, or comment menu, or contact support. You can block another account. Rally may remove content or restrict posting when these rules are broken. Normal posts do not require manual approval.

Automated screening can make mistakes. Contact support if your content was incorrectly blocked. The Founder badge identifies Rally's founder; it does not endorse other people's posts.
